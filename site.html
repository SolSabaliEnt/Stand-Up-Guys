Our Story

This mission is personal.

Born and raised in Detroit, Antonio Washington grew up witnessing environments shaped by violence, drugs, loss, and limited positive guidance.

His story did not end where it started. Antonio continued his education, earned his GED, and entered work supporting the development of young people. What he survived became a way to understand, reach, and guide young people facing many of the same pressures.

Our Mission

Inspire. Guide. Empower. Transform.

Stand Up Guys exists to inspire, guide, empower, and transform young people through trusted mentorship, critical-thinking development, financial literacy, workforce preparation, and positive life experiences.

We build meaningful one-on-one relationships with youth who may feel misunderstood or lack consistent guidance, helping them recognize their potential, make stronger decisions, and develop practical life skills.

Our Vision

A generation prepared to build.

We envision a future where young people have access to the guidance, knowledge, opportunity, and positive relationships needed to build productive and purposeful lives.

The long-term goal is to develop confident, responsible, financially informed, career-ready young men who eventually become mentors and positive examples for the generation that follows.

Who will the first cohort serve?+

The planned Detroit pilot is designed for young men ages 13–18. Enrollment details, referral partners, and the final launch calendar will be announced as the founding semester is funded and organized.

Is Stand Up Guys accepting mentors?+

Stand Up Guys welcomes mentor interest. Anyone working directly with youth will be expected to complete screening, training, and all program safety requirements before being matched with a participant.

What does a founding partnership support?+

Funding supports the complete first-semester experience: staffing, meals, transportation, exposure visits, curriculum, participant support, mentor screening, insurance, facilities, technology, administration, and evaluation.

How will impact be measured?+

The pilot will track participation, retention, mentor connection, practical knowledge, career preparation, exposure experiences, youth goals, and youth and family feedback. Stand Up Guys will report measured results—not estimates presented as outcomes.

Are contributions tax deductible?+

Stand Up Guys is still developing its formal organizational and charitable structure. Tax-deductible status should not be assumed unless it is separately confirmed in writing before a contribution is made.
